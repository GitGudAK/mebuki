Mebuki

See exactly how to care for your plants.

A free iPhone app for people growing plants in small homes, on windowsills and on balconies.

Plant advice is full of phrases like "bright, indirect light" and "well-draining soil", and half of it contradicts the other half. Mebuki shows you instead. Each plant has short animations that show exactly how to water a snake plant, repot a monstera, pinch back basil or feed a chilli, step by step.

Made for small spaces

Every guide is written for apartments: which window, what size pot, how to keep humidity up without a greenhouse, and what to do on a balcony when the weather turns.

Advice for your weather

Tap "Get advice" on a plant and Mebuki checks today's local forecast against what that plant tolerates. It tells you when to bring it in for the night, shade it from a heatwave, raise the humidity or skip watering after rain. It only uses your approximate location, and only when you ask.

Advice you can check

42 plants, from monstera and pothos to tulasi, curry leaf and lemongrass. Every tip links to the source it comes from, such as the Royal Horticultural Society and university extension services, with the ASPCA for pet safety.

Know a better way?

Mebuki is built in the open, so growers can suggest a tip, add a plant or fix something we got wrong. Suggest a tip on GitHub.
