Mebuki

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Mebuki does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

All of Mebuki's content is bundled with the app, and its animations are drawn on your device by an open-source React and SVG engine included in the app. Mebuki makes no network requests of its own and does not send anything you do in the app to us or to anyone else.

Links to other websites

Each guide lists its sources, such as the Royal Horticultural Society, university extension services and the ASPCA. Tapping a source opens that website in your browser, where that site's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Mebuki is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/GitGudAK/mebuki/issues.
