Support

Need help with Niwa, spotted a care tip that looks wrong, or want a plant added?

Open an issue at github.com/GitGudAK/niwa/issues and describe what you saw. For care tips, please include the plant and step.

Common questions

Does Niwa need an internet connection?
No. Everything is bundled with the app, and the animations are drawn on your device by Niwa's own open-source React and SVG engine. Only the source links open websites.

Where does the care advice come from?
Each guide is checked against authoritative sources, listed at the bottom of every guide: the Royal Horticultural Society, university extension services and the ASPCA for pet safety.

Is a plant safe for my cat or dog?
Guides are tagged "pet safe" or "toxic to pets" based on the ASPCA list. If your pet has eaten a plant, contact your vet or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center right away.
