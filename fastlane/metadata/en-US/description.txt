Keep your plants alive, one short guide at a time.

Mebuki is open source plant care for apartment, balcony and windowsill growers. Pick a plant, press play, and watch its care routine unfold step by step: light, water, soil, feeding, pruning, repotting and more. Tap any step to jump straight to it.

42 PLANTS FROM AROUND THE WORLD
Houseplants like Monstera, Pothos, Fiddle Leaf Fig, Boston Fern, Moth Orchid and Bird of Paradise, plus plants people grow everywhere: Tulasi, Curry Leaf and Arabian Jasmine from South Asia, Lemongrass and Pandan, Lucky Bamboo, Juniper Bonsai, Coffee, Rosemary, Meyer Lemon, Chili Pepper and balcony tomatoes.

ADVICE YOU CAN TRUST
Every tip is checked against authoritative sources, listed at the bottom of each guide: the Royal Horticultural Society, university extension services, and the ASPCA for pet safety.

PET SAFETY AT A GLANCE
Filter for pet-safe plants, and see which ones are toxic to cats and dogs before you bring them home.

GROWN IN THE OPEN
Every guide is open source. Gardeners anywhere can suggest a plant or fix a tip, and every improvement grows the app.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Every guide is built into the app and works without a connection.
