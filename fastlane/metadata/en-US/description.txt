Keep your plants alive, one short guide at a time.

Mebuki is open source plant care for apartment, balcony and windowsill growers. Pick a plant, press play, and watch its care routine unfold step by step: light, water, soil, feeding, pruning, repotting and more. Tap any step to jump straight to it.

ADVICE YOU CAN TRUST
Every tip is checked against authoritative sources, listed at the bottom of each guide: the Royal Horticultural Society, university extension services, and the ASPCA for pet safety.
