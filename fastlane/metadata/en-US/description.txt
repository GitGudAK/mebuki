See exactly how to care for your plants.

Plant advice is full of phrases like "bright, indirect light" and "well-draining soil", and half of it contradicts the other half. Mebuki shows you instead. Pick a plant, press play, and watch short animations of exactly what to do: how to water a snake plant, repot a monstera, pinch back basil or feed a chilli. Tap any step to jump straight to it.

MADE FOR SMALL SPACES
Every guide is written for apartments, windowsills and balconies: which window to use, what size pot, how to raise humidity without a greenhouse, and what to do when the weather turns.

ADVICE FOR YOUR WEATHER
Tap "Get advice" on a plant and Mebuki checks today's local forecast against what that plant can take. It tells you when to bring it in for the night, shade it from a heatwave, raise the humidity or skip watering after rain. It uses your approximate location, only when you ask.

42 PLANTS FROM AROUND THE WORLD
Houseplants like Monstera, Pothos, Fiddle Leaf Fig, Boston Fern, Moth Orchid and Bird of Paradise, plus plants people grow everywhere: Tulasi, Curry Leaf and Arabian Jasmine, Lemongrass and Pandan, Lucky Bamboo, Juniper Bonsai, Coffee, Rosemary, Meyer Lemon, Chili Pepper and balcony tomatoes.

ADVICE YOU CAN CHECK
Every tip links to the source it comes from: the Royal Horticultural Society, university extension services, and the ASPCA for pet safety.

PET SAFETY AT A GLANCE
Filter for pet-safe plants, and see which ones are toxic to cats and dogs before you bring them home.

KNOW A BETTER WAY?
Mebuki is built in the open, so growers can suggest a tip or add a plant.

FREE AND PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Every guide is built into the app and works offline.
